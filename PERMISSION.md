# Permission

Total College Experience (TCE) is an unofficial mod of Venus University, made by CrazyyMister.
It includes the original game's art, sounds and characters, so it is distributed with the
original developer's permission.

## What was asked and what was answered

The permission was asked for, and given, by email with Venus Dev (the original developer,
`venus-uni-dev`) on October 9, 2026. The request said:

> A standalone exe would include your art, sounds and characters, and I won't distribute it without your permission

The reply said:

> I have no problem with you sharing that as a standalone mod, especially since you're handling your own bug reports and clearly crediting the original project.

The emails are kept by CrazyyMister and are not published here.

## How this is read

This section is the mod author's reading of the reply, not a legal opinion and not a statement
by Venus Dev.

- The permission is for TCE as a whole, the art, sounds and characters of Venus University
  included, as the mod's download and its source repository.
- It comes with the two things the reply names: TCE's problems are handled by TCE (its own
  Issues, never Venus Dev), and the original project is credited (see `NOTICE` and the Credits
  screen in the game).
- It does not cover the game's music, which TCE does not include, or anyone else reusing the
  original art, sounds or characters in another work. `LICENSE-ASSETS.md` still says what it
  says for those.
